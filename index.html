01 — Where you may be now

Something important is asking for your attention.

The situations vary. They share the difficulty of trying to see clearly while you are inside the system and personally responsible for what happens next.

↗

You are carrying a decision no one inside the company can help you think through freely.

The people around you have roles, loyalties, and stakes in the outcome.

↗

A cofounder or executive relationship is affecting the whole business.

The visible disagreement may be only one part of what is happening between you.

↗

The strategy keeps shifting.

The company has not stayed with a direction long enough to learn whether the plan or the execution is the issue.

↗

The vision still lives mostly in your head.

You have explained it many times, yet people continue to act from different pictures of where the company is going.

↗

The same problems keep returning.

Meetings address the symptoms, people leave with good intentions, and the underlying pattern remains.

↗

You know something in your own leadership needs to change.

An avoided conversation, old assumption, or need for control may be shaping what the business can do next.

02 — The work

A place to see more clearly and work with what you find.

Ongoing strategic clarity

See what is really happening, get clear on what matters, and find a way forward you can stand behind.

You know the business from the inside. My role is to listen closely, make hidden assumptions visible, and notice patterns that are difficult to see while you are living inside them. I offer perspective when I have it while keeping the work grounded in your knowledge, judgment, and responsibility for the choices.

Over time, I come to understand the history, the people, and the patterns that keep returning. That continuity lets each conversation build on the work that came before it.

03 — What changes

The work should become visible in the life of the business.

↗

Decisions reflect a fuller view of the strategic, human, and personal consequences.

You can see the strategic, human, and personal consequences clearly enough to choose.

↗

Difficult conversations address what has been avoided and make movement more possible.

What people have been circling gets named and worked with directly.

↗

Priorities remain stable long enough to learn.

The company can act, track progress, and use execution as information.

↗

Ownership extends beyond the founder.

People understand more of the direction and carry greater responsibility for the work.

↗

Recurring problems are addressed closer to their root.

The business becomes better able to recognize and address the pattern when it returns.

↗

Your attention returns to what only you can carry.

More of your capacity is available for customers, growth, and what comes next.

05 — Who I work with

Founders and executives who care deeply about the company they are building.

My clients tend to lead established businesses with real customers and a growing team, while a great deal still rests on their shoulders.

Some are building companies with an explicit social mission. Others are creating valuable products, good work, and a business they want to be proud of. What matters is a willingness to look closely at the business, listen to perspectives beyond your own, and examine how your leadership is shaping what happens next.
