02 — What it is

Not a strategy deck handed across a table. A passage from stuck to in motion.

Together we step back far enough to see your whole business at once: the forest and the trees, the pieces and the way they fit.

We find the throughline that connects what you do to why you do it, and surface the places where understanding has quietly fractured. Capable people can talk past each other for months because each one is protecting a different piece of the truth. Most of what looks like strategic disagreement is really translation failure, and it doesn't resolve on its own.
