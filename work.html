In detail

What each engagement actually contains.

01 · Ongoing · One-on-one

1:1 Strategic Advisory

A standing thinking partner for the person carrying the weight of the decisions. Some clients call it executive coaching; I call it advisory, because we work on the business as much as the leader.

How it works

An ongoing rhythm. Usually a working session every other week, plus the calls that can't wait for the calendar. Confidential, candid, and with no agenda but yours.

What happens

We pressure-test the hard calls before you announce them. We prepare for the conversations you've been putting off. We read what's actually happening in your team when the meeting says one thing and the room says another. And we keep the mission, the people, and the practical next step in the same frame.

You get

Decisions made sooner and cleaner. A place to think out loud that isn't your board or your team. The steadiness of not carrying it alone. I've held these seats myself; I know how lonely the top can be, and what changes when someone sees the whole board with you.

Best for

A founder or senior leader who needs a trusted, confidential sounding board — someone seeing the whole board alongside them.

02 · 2–6 weeks · Embedded

Strategic Diagnostic

A clear-eyed diagnosis of your team, strategy, and systems, and the thread that turns it into a path forward. Weeks, not quarters.

How it works

Two to six weeks, embedded part-time. I sit in your meetings, interview your key people — sometimes your clients or board — and read the documents that matter.

What happens

I listen for the difference between what gets said and what's meant, and map where understanding has fractured: who is solving which problem, where definitions of success quietly diverge, what everyone has been circling but not saying. Along the way I facilitate the conversations that haven't been had, bringing translation and real presence to the places where capable people have stopped hearing each other.

You receive

A written assessment naming the real pattern. A map of the misalignments. The difficult conversations, already begun and skillfully held. Concrete recommendations delivered in a working session with your leadership, with an optional planning session to turn the diagnosis into a plan.

Best for

A leadership team that senses something's off, or something's possible, and wants an outside eye that actually gets inside.

03 · 1–2 days · Intensive

Transformational Strategy Workshop

One to two days that turn a fragmented room into an aligned one.

How it works

Not an off-the-shelf agenda. I interview every participant beforehand, design the session around your actual moment, and facilitate one to two days with your key people. Thirty days later we reconvene to check the momentum.

What happens

The room builds the picture together: where you actually are, what you're actually for, and the path between them. Disagreements that have circled for months get named and worked. The plan that emerges belongs to everyone who made it.

You leave with

A shared vision the team built. A plan with owners and sequence. The outcomes documented. Momentum that starts the next morning. Best in person, possible virtually. I've designed and led many of these. It works.

Best for

A team ready to move boldly, together, and fast.

How it starts

Not sure which one fits? That's what the first conversation is for.

Most engagements begin with the same simple step: a conversation about where you are and where you're trying to go.

From there we'll find the right shape together. Most of my ongoing work is 1:1 advisory. A diagnostic often opens into a workshop, and a workshop often settles into a standing advisory rhythm. The point isn't to sell you the biggest option. It's to match the work to the moment you're actually in.

Let's talk

Stuck is expensive. Getting unstuck doesn't have to take months.

Tell me what you're wrestling with. Once the real pattern is named, clarity comes faster than most teams expect.
