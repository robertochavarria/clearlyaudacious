Ways to work together

Three ways in, depending on how far you're ready to move.

Each is built on the same foundation — seeing the whole before deciding the next step — and they ascend in scope and intensity.

1:1 Advisory

The Counsel

Ongoing strategy and coaching for founders and senior leaders

A standing thinking partner for the person carrying the weight of the decisions. We meet regularly to clarify direction, pressure-test the hard calls, and keep you tethered to the why. Candor without an agenda, a place to think out loud, and the steadiness of not carrying it alone.

Best forA leader who needs a trusted, confidential sounding board.
Strategic Assessment

The Read

Embedded interviews, a clear-eyed assessment, and a planning session to act on it

I come inside for a stretch — your meetings, interviews with key people, sometimes your clients — and listen for what's really going on beneath what gets said. Then a clear-eyed assessment and concrete recommendations, with an optional planning session to turn them into action.

Best forA team that senses something's off, or something's possible, and wants an outside eye that gets inside.
Strategy Workshop

The Leap

A 1–2 day transformational planning workshop, designed and facilitated by me

A high-intensity session with your key people. In a day or two, a room that walks in fragmented walks out aligned, clear, and lit up — with a plan they built together. Best in person, possible virtually. This is the one that creates a dramatic leap forward in a compressed window. I've designed and led many. It works.

Best forA team ready to move boldly, together, and fast.
How it starts

Not sure which one fits? That's what the first conversation is for.

Most engagements begin with the same simple step: a conversation about where you are and where you're trying to go.

From there we'll find the right shape together — sometimes that's a standing advisory rhythm, sometimes an embedded read, sometimes a workshop that moves everything at once. The point isn't to sell you the biggest option. It's to match the work to the moment you're actually in.
